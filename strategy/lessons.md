# lessons — distilled, active beliefs (read before EVERY decision)

This is the agent's long-term memory. NOTES.md is the raw episodic ledger;
this file is what survives consolidation. The decision stage of every pass
must check entries here before sizing any trade.

## Lifecycle of a lesson
- CANDIDATE — observed 1–2×. Logged here but does NOT bind decisions yet.
- ACTIVE — ≥3 supporting observations (cite the NOTES/SCORECARD rows). Binds:
  a trade that contradicts an ACTIVE lesson needs a written justification.
- RETIRED — contradicted by ≥2 newer observations or by a weekly retro. Kept
  (struck through) so the same lesson isn't relearned from scratch.
One observation = one resolved trade or one validated/falsified news call.
Never promote on a single outcome — that's superstition, not learning.

# people — policy & smart-money watch roster

Who the news sweep monitors, and HOW each signal type is allowed to act.
A person's words are information about policy/flows, not truth about value.
No people-signal ever overrides a rail, skips the playbook, or changes sizing.

## Tier 1 — policy movers (checked EVERY tick; statements move markets in minutes)
| Who | Watch for | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| President (Trump) | Truth Social posts / statements: tariffs, trade deals, threats naming companies or sectors, Fed pressure | Names a held ticker or its sector → classify THESIS-BREAKING-candidate, run position review immediately. Market-wide (tariffs, Fed) → regime input + S5 check. NEVER a buy signal by itself — policy posts reverse. |
| Fed Chair (Powell) + FOMC voters | Speeches, testimony, surprise comments on rates/QT | Regime input. Speech days on calendar.md = treat like macro-event days (no new entries before). |
| Treasury Sec / key regulators (SEC, FTC, antitrust) | Actions naming held/allowlist companies | Held name targeted → THESIS-BREAKING-candidate, review the position this tick. |

## Tier 2 — congressional traders (checked once daily, first tick)
| Who | Watch for | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Pelosi + notable congressional filers | New STOCK Act disclosures (Capitol Trades / Quiver / similar trackers) | **ENTRY-RELEVANT idea source ONLY.** Disclosures lag up to 45 days — the move may be over. A disclosure earns a ticker a watchlist/allowlist *proposal*, then the trade must still qualify under a playbook setup on its own chart. Cluster buys (several members, same name, same window) > a single filing. |

## Tier 3 — market movers & smart money (checked once daily, first tick)
| Who | Watch for | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Musk | Posts moving his own or named companies | Volatility warning on the named ticker; affects held positions only. |
| Buffett/Berkshire, major activists (13F/13D) | New large stakes, activist letters | ENTRY-RELEVANT idea source; 13Fs lag a quarter — idea, not entry. |
| Short-report firms | Reports on held/allowlist names | Held name targeted → THESIS-BREAKING-candidate same tick; don't knee-jerk exit on the headline alone — check the claims, but tighten the invalidation immediately. |

## Hard rules
- Tier-1 sweep happens every tick; Tiers 2–3 on the first tick of the day.
- Log hits to news/YYYY-MM-DD.md with a `person` tag:
  `HH:MM | PERSON(name) | class | one-line | source`
- A people-signal entry idea still needs: playbook setup + own invalidation +
  risk-based size + calendar check. "Pelosi bought it" is not a thesis.
- Rumored/unsourced statements: log as UNVERIFIED, act only after a second
  reputable source or the primary post itself. Exits may act faster than entries.
- Account data never goes into these searches (same rule as all web use).
